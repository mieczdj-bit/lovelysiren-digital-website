# Account and Data Deletion — LovelySiren Digital

**Customer-facing website copy for:** `https://lovelysirendigital.com/delete-account.html`  
**Publication status:** [REPLACE EXISTING PLACEHOLDER ONLY AFTER EXECUTIVE PRODUCER APPROVAL]

LovelySiren Digital respects requests to review, correct or delete personal information associated with our games and account services.

## How to request deletion

Email **[lovelysirenstudios@gmail.com](mailto:lovelysirenstudios@gmail.com)** with the subject **Account or Data Deletion Request**. In your message, tell us:

1. Whether the request concerns **Road Trip Arcade game data**, a **LovelySiren Account**, or **both**.
2. The email address associated with the account, if applicable, and your **RTA Player ID** if you know it.
3. Whether you are requesting deletion of the account and associated data, correction of information, or information about what is stored.

**Do not email us your password, authentication codes, complete payment-card information or private Google Play purchase tokens.** If we need to confirm account or parental authority, we will explain an appropriate verification method.

You can submit a request even if you have uninstalled the game or cannot currently sign in. An existing account is not required merely to contact us.

## What the request can affect

An RTA game-data request may involve online game identity, friends/invitations, multiplayer records, saved and completed game information, and related eligible product data. A LovelySiren Account request may involve company profile data, authentication and permanent identity associations. These are separate systems; requesting removal from one does not automatically remove the other.

We review the affected records, whether they are owned by the verified requester, and whether any information must be retained for a specific lawful purpose, such as purchase verification, fraud prevention, security or an unresolved dispute. The applicable Privacy Policies describe the types of information involved.

## What happens after you contact us

We will review your request, explain any needed identity verification, and tell you whether the requested action is completed, in progress, or subject to a specific limitation. Some requests may require additional technical or operational review. **We will not describe a request as completed until the relevant data operations have been verified.**

Deleting an app from a device or signing out does not by itself remove cloud records. Removing game records and removing a company-wide account may require separate actions.

For general questions about our practices, please see our [Website & LovelySiren Account Privacy Policy](https://lovelysirendigital.com/privacy.html), [Road Trip Arcade Privacy Policy](https://lovelysirendigital.com/games/road-trip-arcade-privacy.html), or [Contact Us](https://lovelysirendigital.com/support.html).
