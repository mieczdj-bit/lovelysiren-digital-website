## 1. What this policy covers

This Privacy Policy explains how LovelySiren Digital ("we," "us," or "our") handles information through **https://lovelysirendigital.com**, its support services, and its company-wide LovelySiren Account system. You can browse public website pages without creating an account. Some account features may be unavailable or limited to invited participants.

Each game may have separate privacy practices. Road Trip Arcade has its own [Road Trip Arcade Privacy Policy](https://lovelysirendigital.com/games/road-trip-arcade-privacy.html). A LovelySiren Account is different from a game's guest identity, Player ID, saved games, and purchase records. Creating a company account does not automatically link or transfer a game's data or purchases.

## 3. Information we collect

The information depends on the features you choose to use:

- **Account registration and profile.** Display name, email address, authentication account identifier, account status, creation/update times, and information needed to keep your account secure. A phone number and mailing address may be offered as optional fields; they are not required to create an account.
- **Age eligibility.** We may ask for a date of birth to determine whether you are at least 18. The company account profile retains an adult-eligibility result, not your full birth date or precise age. Limited information may also be processed to prevent abuse of registration.
- **Authentication.** If you choose email/password sign-in, your password is handled by Firebase Authentication and is not stored in your LovelySiren account profile. If you use Google sign-in, we receive authentication identifiers and account details made available through that sign-in method, such as a verified email address. We do not receive your Google password.
- **Account agreements.** We record the versions of the Website Terms and Privacy Policy that you accept, document fingerprints, and the time our service records acceptance. Acceptance of website policies does not accept any game's separate terms on your behalf.
- **Account identification.** After successful registration or enrollment, we associate the verified Firebase authentication account with a unique, permanent LovelySiren ID. This is separate from a game's Player ID and other game-specific identifiers.
- **Temporary security records.** We use limited invitations, registration grants, identity reservations, authentication-related status records, and abuse-prevention information such as keyed or hashed email/network identifiers, timestamps, and rate limits. Hashed or keyed identifiers may still be personal information when they can be linked to a person.
- **Messages to us.** If you email us, we receive the sender address, message and any details you include. If the Contact Us form becomes available, it collects a name, reply email, selected product or topic, optional subject information and message. Please do not include passwords, verification codes, full payment-card details, or unnecessary sensitive information.
- **Technical information.** Our hosting, authentication and security providers may process IP addresses, browser/device information, request timestamps, error/security logs, and similar technical information needed to operate and protect the services.

An interrupted registration or an attempted Google sign-in may create an authentication record or a temporary registration record without completing a LovelySiren Account. Such records are reviewed under our security and retention procedures; an unsuccessful registration does not necessarily erase them immediately.

## 4. How we use the information

We use information to provide requested website and account services; determine eligibility; authenticate and verify users; maintain a consistent account identifier; provide account settings and recovery; send necessary verification, security and service messages; record policy acceptance; answer support and privacy requests; protect against fraud and abuse; troubleshoot problems; and meet applicable legal obligations.

**A LovelySiren ID alone does not prove ownership or authorize access.** The company account service verifies credentials, account status and applicable permissions. An authorized game service may request an existing LovelySiren ID when a supported integration is enabled, but looking up that ID does not itself grant purchases, entitlements, device access, or ownership of game data.

## 5. Sign-in, verification and account linking

LovelySiren Accounts may support email/password and Google authentication. Registration can be restricted to invitations. Email verification may be required before protected account functions are available. Firebase handles password reset and email-verification messages; those links can include sensitive action codes.

When an existing Road Trip Arcade Google user explicitly enrolls, the company service checks the verified Firebase user identity and requires the authorized enrollment process. We do **not** automatically merge accounts, Player IDs, purchases or game records merely because email addresses match. Choosing a different Google identity may result in a rejected company enrollment and an authentication record that requires reconciliation.

An authenticated session may be remembered in browser storage by Firebase. Limited interface and request-cooldown information may also be stored during a browser session. Signing out ends that browser's current session; it does not delete cloud records or automatically sign you out of every device.

## 6. Optional contact details and promotions

Providing a telephone number or mailing address does not sign you up for marketing. A phone number is not a verified phone-based sign-in or password-recovery method.

If promotional preferences are offered, **email, text message and postal promotions are independent choices and each starts off by default**. Account registration, policy acceptance, purchase activity and support requests are not marketing opt-ins. You may withdraw an enabled choice independently without closing your account.

Promotional text messaging is not active unless and until we separately introduce it with the applicable consent, number-verification, sender and opt-out protections. Where promotional email is sent, we will identify the sender and provide an unsubscribe method. We do not sell contact information or provide it to other businesses for their own independent marketing.

Website account preferences do not necessarily control offers shown inside a game; game-specific advertising and offers are covered by that game's practices.

## 7. Contact Us and support messages

Our support page provides an email contact method. A web contact form may also be available. When available, the form may use a delivery service to send the message to our support mailbox and a bot-protection service to reduce automated abuse. Those providers may process the message details or device/network verification signals needed for their assigned purpose.

Sending a support message does **not** establish account ownership, prove a purchase, provide marketing consent, or authorize an account change or deletion. We may ask you to complete additional verification through an appropriate channel for sensitive requests; we will not ask you to email your password or one-time verification code.

For privacy or safety concerns involving a child, a parent or guardian may contact us using the address above. We will review the request and limit or remove information when appropriate under applicable requirements.

We use service providers to operate our website and, when enabled, account services. These include:

- **GitHub Pages**, for public website hosting.
- **Google Firebase Authentication and Firebase Realtime Database**, for account authentication and the related account records when the service is enabled.
- **Google and Firebase authentication/email services**, for selected sign-in, email verification and password recovery.
- **Google Gmail**, for our support mailbox.
- **Additional email-delivery or bot-protection providers**, if those support features are activated; their identities and handling will be reflected in this policy when used.

Providers may process the information needed to deliver their services under their own applicable terms and notices, potentially in the United States or other locations where they operate. We do not promise that all information is stored in one geographic region.

We may also disclose information when reasonably necessary to comply with a lawful obligation; respond to an appropriately verified request; prevent fraud or harm; protect service security or legal rights; or carry out a lawful business transfer with appropriate safeguards. We do not sell customer contact information or share it for independent third-party marketing.

We do not currently run a separate website behavioral-advertising program. If this changes, we will update applicable disclosures before using it.

## 9. Data retention

We keep information only while there is a legitimate service, security, support, dispute-resolution or legal reason for doing so. Different records may have different review or deletion periods. Active account records and identity associations generally remain while an account is maintained; optional contact fields may be updated or removed through supported controls. Some limited records may need to remain after closure to document an action, address a dispute, prevent unauthorized reuse or meet a lawful obligation.

Temporary registration grants have short validity windows; invitations and rate-limit records also have expiration rules. **Expiration is not the same as immediate deletion from storage.** Actual cleanup, provider logs, support mailbox content and backups follow their applicable review and removal processes. Account closure may require coordination with any linked game and providers; deleting a local app, signing out, or disabling login does not erase all account data.

If we receive personal information from a child in a context where it should not have been collected or retained, we will review it promptly and remove or limit it as required for the specific purpose and applicable law.

## 10. Protecting information

We use access restrictions, authenticated account operations, limited privileges, security checks and other reasonable measures to protect the information we handle. Only authorized services or operators should access private account records. No internet service or storage system can promise absolute security.

Protect your login credentials and email account, and contact us if you suspect unauthorized access.

## 11. Your choices and privacy requests

You may request information about your data, correction of inaccurate information, withdrawal of optional marketing choices, or deletion where applicable. Some changes can be made in Account Settings when that service is available.

For help, contact **lovelysirenstudios@gmail.com** or see **https://lovelysirendigital.com/delete-account.html**. Please identify the service involved and describe the request without sending passwords or verification codes. We may need to verify your authority before disclosing data or performing sensitive actions. Rights, verification standards and lawful retention exceptions depend on the request and applicable law.

Company account deletion and deletion of Road Trip Arcade game data are different operations. A request affecting both must be reviewed across both services. Until automated deletion features are available, requests are handled through the stated support process. We will not describe data as erased unless the relevant operations are verified; if a part cannot be completed, we will explain its status and any applicable retention or technical limitations.

## 12. Children and eligibility

Independent LovelySiren Account registration is limited to adults **18 or older**. We do not currently offer child or parent-managed LovelySiren Accounts. That restriction does not mean that all LovelySiren games are adults-only; each game has its own audience and privacy practices.

If you believe a child provided information in connection with an unauthorized company account, please contact us so we can review and address it.

## 13. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when services or practices change. The current version and effective date will appear at the top of the published policy. Where required, we will provide notice or request new acceptance before continuing a protected account feature. This policy does not replace a game's separate privacy policy or terms.
