## 3. Registration and information you provide

When account registration is available, we process:

- Your chosen display name. It need not be your legal name. Your private company profile does not automatically change a game's player name.
- Your email address for authentication, verification, recovery and necessary account communications.
- An optional telephone number, if provided. It is private profile information; we do not verify it or use it for phone sign-in, account recovery, SMS or marketing calls. Providing a number is not consent to marketing calls or texts.
- Your date of birth to determine whether you meet the company-account requirement of age 18 or older. We do not retain your full birth date or exact age in account records; eligible registrations retain an adult eligibility result. Ineligible applicants do not receive a company account or permanent LovelySiren ID. Limited security information may still be processed to prevent abuse.
- Password credentials when you choose email/password authentication. During registration, your password passes over the secure account service to Firebase Authentication. We do not store it in your account profile. Later password sign-in and recovery are handled through Firebase Authentication. Do not send passwords or verification codes to support.
- Your acceptance of the applicable published Website Terms and Privacy Policy, including document versions, content fingerprints and the time acceptance is recorded by our service. Company-account acceptance does not accept a game's agreement on your behalf.
- Information you choose to include in support messages and information needed to respond to your request.

An interrupted registration can leave an unverified authentication record, a reserved identifier or temporary registration information before a full company profile is completed. These records are subject to review and authorized cleanup; an unsuccessful registration does not mean all information has automatically been erased.

## 4. Permanent LovelySiren IDs and account records

Firebase Authentication assigns an authentication identifier. Our company account service associates it with a permanent LovelySiren ID and private profile. These identifiers are distinct from your email address and a game's friendly Player ID. A permanent identifier supports stable account ownership; possession of an identifier alone does not authorize access.

Account records include your display name, optional telephone number, adult eligibility result, account and verification state, creation and update times, policy-acceptance history and identity associations. Registration and security processing also use invitation records, temporary authorization records, reserved identifiers and security counters. Some keys are derived from email addresses or network information using hashing. Hashing does not necessarily make information anonymous.

Company-account access requires authorized authentication, completed account setup and verified email. When applicable policies change, affected services may require renewed acceptance. The company identity service has been prepared but is not currently available to games or other applications. If made available, it will check an existing account's authentication, eligibility, policy acceptance and status before returning its company identifier to an authorized service; an ordinary lookup will not create an account.

We do not currently migrate game identities, grant purchases, change game Player IDs or connect game records through this company-account service. Any future connection will require the applicable information and safeguards before it is introduced.

## 5. Email/password, Google and recovery

We use Google Firebase Authentication for account authentication and Firebase Realtime Database for private company-account records. Verification and password-reset messages use Firebase's email services. Links may contain sensitive action codes; protect them like account credentials. Recovery responses are designed not to disclose whether an email address has an account.

Email/password registration and Google account features will be available only when offered by us. If Google sign-in or linking is offered, Google provides authentication identifiers and relevant account information, such as an email address. We do not receive your Google password. Linking requires appropriate authenticated verification; matching email addresses do not automatically merge accounts or purchases. Google's own privacy information also applies to its services.

We do not currently provide phone-based sign-in, Apple sign-in or public player chat through the website account service.

## 6. Technical information and local sessions

Hosting and account providers receive information needed to deliver and secure their services, such as IP address, browser and device information, request timing and network information. Our account service uses email- and IP-derived security counters to limit abuse and checks authentication credentials, account state and policy acceptance to protect access. Authorized backend-service access is separately checked; a user token alone does not authorize a privileged service.

Our application is designed to avoid logging raw request bodies, passwords, birth dates and authentication tokens. Providers may maintain operational and security logs under their service settings. We do not maintain a separate website history of successful or attempted logins; Firebase processes sign-in, failed sign-in, verification and recovery requests and related technical information.

When account services are enabled, Firebase stores authentication session information in your browser so a session can be restored after a refresh. Some presentation and request-cooldown information may also be stored for the browser session. Signing out ends the application's current authenticated session; it does not delete your account or cloud records. Browser storage controls can remove local information and may affect session restoration. Local session storage is not advertising consent or authorization by itself.

## 9. Retention and security

We retain information for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the services you use, administer accounts, protect security, resolve disputes and meet applicable legal obligations. Different record categories may require different retention. We do not promise one deletion period for all records.

Temporary registration authorizations expire after 15 minutes, and temporary provider-link authorizations expire after 5 minutes. Invitations and security counters have their own expiration times. Expiration prevents further use of an authorization; it does not itself erase the stored record. Removal depends on a separately authorized cleanup process, and no automatic cleanup schedule is currently operating.

Account profiles, permanent identity associations, policy-acceptance history and identifier reservations are not removed by temporary-record cleanup. Provider logs and backups may be retained separately and may not be removed immediately when current records are removed. Some information may need to be retained for lawful security, fraud-prevention, transaction, dispute or other legal purposes. We will explain applicable limitations when handling a request.

We use access restrictions and other reasonable security measures. No electronic transmission or storage method is completely secure. Protect your email account and credentials, secure your devices and contact us if you suspect compromise.

## 10. Access, correction and deletion requests

Where enabled, verified account holders can view their own account information and edit supported profile fields, including removing an optional telephone number. Contact lovelysirenstudios@gmail.com to request access, correction, deletion or other applicable privacy rights. We may request reasonable identity verification before releasing information or making a sensitive change. Do not send your password.

Deletion information is available at https://lovelysirendigital.com/delete-account.html. Automated company-account deletion and the trusted Road Trip Arcade game-data deletion service are not currently available. Requests require verified scope and coordinated handling; submitting a request, signing out or disabling an account does not mean records have been erased.

Deleting company-account information is distinct from deleting a game's data or local saves. A request to delete only Road Trip Arcade data does not delete the company-wide LovelySiren Account. Company-wide requests need coordination with affected products. Applicable purchase records, platform entitlements, lawful retention and effects on access must be considered rather than assumed. We will explain incomplete actions and applicable limitations; we will not describe partial deletion as completed deletion.

Depending on applicable law and your circumstances, you may have rights to access, correction, deletion, portability, restrictions on processing or other rights and complaint options. Contact us about a request or an applicable response or appeal. We assess requests under the law that applies. Nothing in this policy limits non-waivable rights.

## 11. Children and game guest features

Independent company-account registration is restricted to people age 18 or older. We do not offer child company accounts or parent-managed company accounts. Contact us if you believe a child has supplied personal information through an unauthorized company account.

Eligible children may use supported game guest features without creating an adult company account, subject to the game's applicable eligibility rules and privacy disclosures. Company-account eligibility is separate from a game's guest access, age treatment and advertising safeguards. This policy does not make games adults-only or replace product-specific child-privacy protections.
