3. Road Safety — Important

Road Trip Arcade is entertainment, not a driving aid or navigation application.

The safety of drivers, passengers, pedestrians, and other road users must always take priority over gameplay.

Drivers must not operate, view, or interact with the Game while driving if doing so would distract them or violate applicable laws.

Some activities permit driver participation without direct interaction with the device. For example, a driver may verbally answer questions, serve as a referee, or participate in Music Wheel while a passenger operates the device.

Such participation is appropriate only when it does not interfere with safe vehicle operation.

Passengers should control the device whenever the vehicle is moving.

Players must not:

  • Encourage a driver to look at or handle a device while driving.
  • Distract a driver with gameplay, arguments, or demands for immediate responses.
  • Unfasten seat belts or move unsafely around a moving vehicle to complete an activity.
  • Leave a vehicle in an unsafe location to locate an object.
  • Enter restricted property, trespass, or take unnecessary risks to complete a game.
  • Violate traffic laws or other safety requirements in pursuit of game objectives.

If a game activity cannot be completed safely, skip it or wait until the vehicle is safely parked.

Players are responsible for exercising reasonable judgment and complying with applicable laws.

LovelySiren Digital does not encourage unsafe driving, distracted driving, or hazardous behavior and does not accept responsibility for conduct outside its control, except where applicable law provides otherwise.

4. Available Games

Road Trip Arcade includes seven games:

  1. Scavenger Hunt — Find designated objects during your travels.
  2. Road Bingo — Complete bingo patterns by spotting objects.
  3. License Plate Hunt — Find license plates representing the 50 U.S. states.
  4. 20 Questions — Discover the mystery answer through questions and clues.
  5. ABC's — Play category-based alphabet challenges.
  6. Music Wheel — Enjoy music-related challenges and activities.
  7. Roadblock — Solve word challenges while managing your remaining misses.

Individual games may offer different rules, player configurations, content libraries, timers, difficulty options, or other features.

The Game provides instructions and available settings for individual activities.

You agree to follow the applicable in-game rules and to use the Game in a fair and reasonable manner.
