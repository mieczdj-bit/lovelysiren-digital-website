## Proposed customer-facing disclosure

When the contact form is available, you can contact LovelySiren Digital without an account or email application. We collect the name, reply email, product category, any requested product/subject details and message you provide, to receive, route and respond to your support request. Please do not include passwords, verification codes, full payment-card details or unnecessary sensitive information.

We use trusted email-delivery and bot-verification providers to process information necessary to deliver support messages and protect the form from abuse. The email provider processes the submitted details for delivery to our support mailbox. A bot-verification service may process challenge and device/network information under its reviewed configuration. Our hosting infrastructure may also process connection/security metadata. Provider identities, safeguards, processing locations and applicable disclosures will be reviewed before the form is activated.

The form does not subscribe you to promotional communications or change email, text or postal marketing preferences. We do not sell contact information or provide it to other companies for their independent marketing. Trusted providers may process necessary data on our behalf to provide their services, subject to appropriate safeguards.

We use limited anti-abuse information, such as keyed fingerprints of submissions, network/email rate-limit keys, timestamps and acceptance/retry state, to limit abuse and duplicate messages. These records do not need a second copy of your message text. Keyed fingerprints are still treated as personal information where linkable; they are not a promise of anonymity. Application logging is designed to exclude raw message text and contact details. Platform/provider connection logs are separately governed by reviewed operational settings.

A support message does not establish account or purchase ownership and does not by itself authorize deletion, billing adjustments, access to private information or account changes. Such actions require independent verification. Provider acceptance confirms receipt for delivery processing, not final delivery to our inbox.

Support messages and replies may remain in the support mailbox and approved delivery provider systems under reviewed support, security, legal-hold and retention procedures. We keep anti-abuse and submission-status records only as necessary for their purposes; unresolved delivery records may require manual review before removal to avoid duplicate delivery. Contact us at lovelysirenstudios@gmail.com about applicable access, correction or deletion rights. We may need to verify your identity independently.
