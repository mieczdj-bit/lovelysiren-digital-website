# Contact service security and cross-project risks

## Safeguards and data minimization

- Allowlisted body fields/products only; conditional subject required for Other and Billing/Purchases; name100, email254, detail160, message5000, bot token4096 characters, raw JSON32KiB. Header/control injection is rejected; mail is plain text with a fixed subject/category and recipient. Visitor email is Reply-To, not From. No attachments or autoresponder/open relay.
- Shared persistent transactional store, separate from account/marketing/game data. Pre-bot attempt limits:10/IP/hour,5/email/hour and200 total/hour. Global cap is checked before allocating arbitrary new key records; caps are preview defaults requiring operational review. CORS/Origin, honeypot and native browser validation supplement server checks, never replace bot verification/quotas.
- Store contains keyed IP/email/payload/request-ID fingerprints, quota counts, status, lease and timestamps only; no raw message/name/email/IP/bot token. Stable private HMAC secret is required. Browser retains unchanged-attempt ID only in memory; no contact values in URLs or browser persistent storage. Provider/mailbox necessarily receive the support message and need separate retention/access controls.
- Accepted payload duplicates are suppressed for24h, including a different browser request ID. Reused IDs cannot alter payload. Pending leases prevent overlapping dispatch. Expired or ambiguous work becomes unknown and cannot resend automatically. Explicit provider nonacceptance can retry after30s. Uncertain database acknowledgement cannot produce frontend success; a later retry may observe the durable accepted receipt.
- Quota/accepted/retryable/request metadata expires logically on access after its configured horizon (hour or24h). No claim that a scheduler physically purges idle data. Pending/unknown receipts stay for reviewed reconciliation; they are not blindly erased after TTL. Hard caps10,000 receipts/20,000 request references stop new claims instead of unbounded growth. A reviewed maintenance/retention/reconciliation process is a live-launch prerequisite; none is activated here.
- No raw PII/error bodies/provider response/request headers/tokens are logged by application code. Public failures are sanitized. Infrastructure/provider access logs and retention still require explicit review. Support data never updates marketingPreferences, identity, purchases or game records.

## Significant Discoveries and Cross-Project Risks

**CONFIRMED functional limitation:** the original support.html offered only a mailto link, leaving visitors without a configured email application no form path. New responsive form and separate service provide that path in local mock testing; production remains honestly unavailable until approved. Five-width browser tests exercise conditional fields, keyboard access, pending/accepted/rejected states and no account/network dependency. This is not a claim the live site already accepts form messages.

**CONFIRMED fault-injection behavior:** a delivery can be accepted while the persistence acknowledgement fails, or an RPC can complete after a lease expires. Blind retries would risk duplicate support mail. The service records durable attempt/lease/status, requires acceptance plus durable acknowledgement for success, and blocks uncertain retries. Tests prove acknowledgement-loss recovery, duplicate/concurrent requests and expired/unknown states. This pattern also matters to future account/security email jobs and game mailbox relays; no defect in existing RTA source is alleged or changed.

**POTENTIAL abuse/availability risk:** distributed bots can spoof Origin, rotate IP/email, or consume quotas to deny other visitors. Shared pre-bot limits cap verification/provider load; verified challenges are still required. Global throttles can reduce availability, especially behind shared NAT. Production edge limits, proxy trust, bot-provider replay protection, rate tuning, monitoring and administrative incident procedures remain review gates. This is not a DDoS-proof claim.

**POTENTIAL privilege/privacy risk:** using an existing broad Firebase Admin or account runtime for public support could expose unrelated customer/game state. The contact entry/package is isolated and initializes no account app; its future least-privilege store/runtime must be reviewed before wiring. Missing account links or a visitor-supplied email are not identity evidence. Billing/account/deletion work always needs independent verification; email signatures and product choices cannot authorize actions.

**POTENTIAL operational risk:** provider acceptance is not inbox delivery; uncertain requests, secret rotation, provider retention, mailbox access and bounced messages require authorized reconciliation. The active adapter retains unknown status rather than inventing success. There is no production requeue/erase-unknown endpoint; provider idempotency has finite retention. Shared account/game workers must not infer support status as purchase verification, deletion completion or marketing consent.

The original published Privacy/Terms/CNAME, all account sources, RTA source and purchases remain unchanged. New disclosure text is a separate review-only draft. No regulatory compliance or live deliverability claim is made.
