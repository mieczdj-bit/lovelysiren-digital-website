**Status: recommendations for decision; unapproved.** This sheet consolidates Pass 009/010 decisions and current source notes. It invents no statutory period and authorizes no collection, hold or deletion. Obtain professional legal review where legal duties, rights, or retention bases are uncertain. “Owner” below means the proposed operational controller, not a legal determination.

## Decisions required before the first account

1. Approve the purpose and necessity of each collected field, especially optional phone, address, adult result and permanent mapping.
2. Assign a named operator and review/removal trigger for each category; determine mailbox, platform log and backup controls.
3. Approve support request identity verification/escalation and what must never be requested over email.
4. Decide how to handle an interrupted registration and test records without deleting unrelated RTA/Auth data.
5. Obtain legal review of applicable rights, required records, holds and any retention duties. This table is a business-process proposal, not a legal retention schedule.
6. Keep company deletion blocked until production company controls and RTA's trusted inventory/fencing/deletion operation are implemented and verified. Never use ad hoc data removal as a substitute.
