Family-Friendly Road-Trip Fun

Road Trip Arcade is designed for family road trips, with curated game content, private play with friends and family, and no public chat or stranger matchmaking.

Play on your own, pass a phone among passengers, or join in as a group. A passenger can read prompts aloud and handle the screen so companions can take part verbally.

Drivers must keep their attention on the road and never handle the app while driving. Verbal participation is only appropriate when it does not distract from driving; otherwise, leave the game to passengers.

Entertainment First, With a Little Learning Along the Way

Road Trip Arcade isn't an educational app, but we've worked useful extras into the fun. Roadblock includes word definitions, 20 Questions shares interesting facts, and License Plate Hunt includes information about the states you find.
