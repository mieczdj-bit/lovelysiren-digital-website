Road Trip Arcade — Privacy Policy

Publisher: LovelySiren Digital
Version: 1.1
Document ID: rta-privacy-v1.1
Effective date: 2026-11-04

1. About this policy

This Privacy Policy explains how Road Trip Arcade ("RTA," "the game") and its publisher, LovelySiren Digital ("we," "us," or "our"), handle information when you use the game. The features available to you can differ by app version, device, game mode, account status, and whether you choose an online feature.

RTA and a company-wide LovelySiren Account are distinct services. You may be able to play supported game modes as a guest, without a company account. The company account service is intended for adults age 18 or older; this is not an age rating for RTA itself. The separate Website & LovelySiren Account Privacy Policy applies to the website and company accounts.

3. Information on your device

To run and resume game activities, RTA may store information on your device, including game preferences, selected games, boards, seeds, progress, found items or states, timers, sound settings, saved or completed games, and other information needed for a chosen feature.

Local information may remain until it is removed through supported game controls, device storage settings, or app removal. Clearing app data or uninstalling may erase local progress; it does not necessarily remove records stored in an online game service or by an external provider. Device operating-system backup and restore settings may affect local data.

4. Sign-in, Player IDs and online identity

Where supported, RTA may use Google Sign-In and Google Firebase Authentication for signed-in features. These providers may process authentication identifiers and account details provided during sign-in, such as your email address and display name. We do not receive your Google password.

RTA maintains its own game identity information, which may include a Firebase user identifier, a game Player ID, profile or display name, and product-specific account references. A Player ID can be used to help other players find or invite the appropriate game account when that feature is available. Game identity information is separate from your email address and from a permanent company-wide LovelySiren ID.

If a supported feature allows you to connect an existing RTA identity with a LovelySiren Account, that connection requires authenticated authorization. Merely using the same email address does not automatically merge player identities, transfer progress, or reassign purchases. Linking a company identity is not proof of ownership of a different game's data.

5. Game activity and multiplayer

Depending on the game modes and online features you use, RTA may handle game setup, selected boards, progress, completion results, game history, player preferences, and related information necessary to resume play or coordinate a session.

If you use supported online multiplayer, friend or invitation features, the game service may handle game identity, friend/invitation relationships, session membership, participation/presence status, game setup and results, and information needed to coordinate the game. Other participating players may see the Player ID or display name and game/session information needed to play together. Information you share as part of a game may be visible to invited participants.

If you submit a suggestion or feedback through a game feature, we may receive the text, associated game or category, relevant account or Player ID and submission time. Please avoid including passwords, payment-card numbers, location details, or other people's private information in suggestions or game messages.

We do not describe RTA as offering public chat or public matchmaking unless those features are actually available in the version you use.

6. Advertising and offers

Some versions or modes may display advertising, including banner advertisements or optional rewarded advertisements, through an approved advertising provider such as Google AdMob. Some game screens may instead contain unfilled advertising spaces or no advertising at all.

When an advertising service is active, it may process information such as an advertising or app identifier, device and network details, approximate location derived from network information, ad requests, interactions, and technical diagnostics, subject to the provider's configuration and policies. Ads that are described as nonpersonalized may still require limited technical information for delivery, security or measurement.

Age-appropriate advertising safeguards apply where required. We do not intend to serve personalized or behaviorally targeted advertisements to children where prohibited. The information used for advertising and the available ad formats depend on the version of the game, its configured services, and the age-related safeguards that apply to the audience.

In-game offers or promotions for LovelySiren Digital products are distinct from optional email, SMS or postal marketing preferences on the company website. Such offers will be handled in accordance with applicable audience and advertising requirements.

7. Purchases and access to paid content

Where RTA offers paid content, Google Play may handle the purchase transaction and payment processing under its own terms and privacy practices. We do not need your complete payment-card number to verify an eligible purchase.

The game may process purchase verification results and limited transaction references, receipt or purchase-token information, account ownership references, and entitlement status for supported purchases, refunds or restores. These records help protect against duplicate claims, fraud and unauthorized access. Paid content remains subject to the purchase and entitlement rules applicable to that product; a company account lookup does not by itself grant a purchase or transfer it between players.

Actual purchase availability depends on the current game version and the applicable app store. A preview, local entitlement cache, or unreleased store feature does not mean a real transaction has occurred.

8. Service providers and technical information

Depending on the features activated for the version you use, RTA may rely on Google Firebase (authentication and game-related storage), Google Sign-In, Google Play services and Billing, and Google AdMob or another disclosed advertising service.

These services may process IP addresses, device/app identifiers and versions, authentication information, technical logs, game or session records, purchase-related data, and advertising signals as needed to provide their functions. Their own notices and privacy terms may also apply. The information processed depends on the SDKs actually enabled in the released app. We do not claim to collect precise device location, contacts, microphone recordings, or other sensitive permissions unless a released feature expressly requires and discloses them.

We may share the minimum information needed with a service provider for authentication, game operations, purchase verification, advertising when enabled, safety or support. We do not sell player contact information for another company's independent marketing.

9. Children and family play

RTA is designed for shared road-trip entertainment. Its game audience and online features are separate from the 18+ requirement for independent LovelySiren Account registration. An adult company account is not a child account and does not automatically grant permission for a child to use online account features.

When a version of RTA is offered to children, applicable restrictions on personal information, advertising, online interaction, and parental consent must be applied to that version. For children or users whose ages are unknown where these rules apply, we restrict the use of personalized advertising and other data practices as required by the applicable platform rules and law. Parents should supervise children's access to online features and avoid placing a child's name or private information into publicly visible identifiers or feedback.

A parent or guardian may contact lovelysirenstudios@gmail.com to ask what information may be associated with their child, to request review or deletion of eligible information, or to withdraw consent where such consent applies. We may verify parental authority before sharing or modifying private records and will limit the collection and retention of children's personal information to purposes and periods allowed for the applicable feature.

10. How long information is kept

Different information serves different purposes. Device-only game state may stay on the device until the user or device removes it. Online account, friend, multiplayer, game progress and purchase records may be kept while needed to provide the chosen features and for reasonable security, support, dispute and legitimate legal purposes.

Completed or abandoned session records and temporary identifiers are subject to cleanup and review procedures rather than indefinite use for an unrelated purpose. Purchase and anti-fraud records may need different treatment from saved games. Provider logs and backups follow their relevant access and retention controls. We do not promise that removing a local save, closing a session, signing out, or uninstalling immediately erases every cloud record.

Where personal information is collected from a child, we limit retention to the time necessary for the specific feature or lawful purpose and apply the relevant children's-data deletion rules. Parents and guardians may contact us for information about the records and retention practices associated with a child’s game features.

11. Security and your choices

We use reasonable protective measures, including account checks, access controls, permissions and service security features. No system can guarantee complete security. Do not share authentication codes or passwords with anyone claiming to represent LovelySiren Digital.

Where game settings permit, you may adjust your local preferences, remove local saved information, or sign out. Some online changes and deletion requests require identity verification to protect the owner and other players.

12. Privacy and deletion requests

For a question, access request, correction request or data-deletion request, contact lovelysirenstudios@gmail.com or visit https://lovelysirendigital.com/delete-account.html. Tell us whether your request concerns Road Trip Arcade game data, a LovelySiren Account, or both, and provide your Player ID if you know it. Do not send your password, private purchase tokens or verification codes.

We may need to establish account or parental authority before making a sensitive change. Deleting RTA data is different from deleting a company-wide account, and neither action should be represented as completed merely because a user signed out, removed the app or had access disabled. Some limited transaction, fraud, security or legally required records may need to be retained for an appropriate purpose, which we will explain where applicable.

We will identify any portion of a deletion request that remains pending, unavailable or subject to a legitimate retention exception. Complete automatic removal of all online RTA records is not currently available. We will provide an accurate status for a request rather than treating the request itself as proof that records have been erased.

13. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as game features, providers or requirements change. The published version and effective date identify the applicable notice. Significant changes will be handled in accordance with applicable notice or consent requirements. This policy is separate from the Website & LovelySiren Account Privacy Policy and from the game's Terms of Service.
