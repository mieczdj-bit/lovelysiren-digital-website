For independent creators

Publish with LovelySiren

LovelySiren Digital plans to work with people who have developed worthwhile game and application concepts. We hope to support projects for mobile, web, PC, and other appropriate platforms as a publisher and development partner.

Ideas deserve a thoughtful look

We value clear concepts, considered design, and creators who can explain what they want to build. A future publishing relationship would be reviewed personally by the Executive Producer. There is no automatic approval or project selection system, and the program is not currently accepting applications.
