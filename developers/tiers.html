Qualifying Tier 4 direct sequels

The stated framework for a qualifying direct sequel to a Tier 4 title is 10% creator / 90% LovelySiren Net Receipts.

A direct sequel is distinct from an unrelated product that happens to share mechanics or a genre. The complete agreement must define eligibility and decision rights; this preview adds no extra test or contract rule.
