Proposal Preparation Guide

A complete, organized proposal helps someone understand the project, what it needs, how far it has progressed, and what kind of publishing relationship you are looking for.

Project overview

  • Project title and overview: Give the project a working title and describe the central idea in a few clear paragraphs.
  • Intended platform: Identify mobile, web, PC, console, or other target platforms and any platform-specific requirements.
  • Target audience: Describe who the project is for and what they might enjoy or need from it.
  • Purpose or player experience: Explain what users will do, learn, feel, or accomplish.
  • Core mechanics or functionality: Describe the essential interactions and how the product works.
  • Features and requirements: Separate essential features from ideas that could be added later.

Development and supporting material

  • Development status: Say whether the project is an idea, concept, prototype, active build, or complete product.
  • Game bible or application specification: Include organized design and behavior details appropriate to the project.
  • Art and sound requirements: Describe the visual direction, audio needs, and any assets already prepared.
  • Screenshots, prototypes, or demos: Provide representative examples when available, with context about what is finished.
  • Technical dependencies: Note engines, services, SDKs, licenses, accounts, devices, or third-party systems the project relies on.
  • Development risks: Identify open questions, hard technical problems, licensing uncertainty, or schedule dependencies.
  • Intellectual-property considerations: Describe the origin of the concept and any third-party material, collaborators, or rights that need review.

Publishing relationship

Explain what kind of support you hope for and what role you would like to take: concept creator, active developer, collaborator, or another clearly described role. Do not assume an ownership split, royalty treatment, buyout, payment schedule, or approval before a written agreement is reviewed and signed.

Before sharing private or sensitive material, wait for an approved submission process and any required confidentiality arrangement. This preview does not accept or store project details.
