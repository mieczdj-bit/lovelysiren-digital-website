Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 5, 2026

LovelySiren Digital develops mobile applications and games, including Road Trip Arcade. This Privacy Policy explains how information may be collected, used, stored, and shared when you use our applications and related services.

Information We May Collect

Depending on the features you use, our apps may process information such as:

  • Account information provided through Google Sign-In, such as an account identifier and email address.
  • Game or application data, such as multiplayer session information, game progress, settings, and other information necessary to provide app features.
  • Information stored locally on your device for settings, preferences, saved progress, and app functionality.
  • Device, advertising, diagnostic, or technical information collected by third-party services used by the app.

How We Use Information

Information is used only as reasonably necessary to operate, maintain, improve, secure, and support our applications. This may include:

  • Providing account and multiplayer features.
  • Saving game progress and preferences.
  • Synchronizing gameplay between participating users.
  • Providing advertising-supported features.
  • Troubleshooting errors and maintaining app security.
  • Responding to support requests.

Third-Party Services

Our applications may use services provided by third parties. These services may process information according to their own privacy policies.

Services currently used or planned for Road Trip Arcade include:

  • Google Sign-In
  • Google Firebase
  • Google AdMob
  • Google Play services

These providers may collect device information, identifiers, account information, network information, diagnostic information, or other data required to provide their services.

Advertising

Some LovelySiren Digital apps may display advertisements, including banner advertisements and optional rewarded advertisements. Advertising services may process device or advertising identifiers and related technical information in order to provide, measure, secure, or personalize advertising where permitted.

Data Storage and Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect data handled by our applications. Data transmitted to supported online services is handled through the security mechanisms provided by those services.

No method of electronic transmission or storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Data Retention

Information is retained only for as long as reasonably necessary to provide application features, maintain required records, resolve disputes, prevent abuse, or comply with applicable legal obligations.

Locally stored information may remain on a device until it is removed through application settings, application removal, device controls, or another applicable deletion method.
